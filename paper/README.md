## Journal requirements checked on 2026-07-20

- Research article; concise English.
- Abstract of 150--250 words.
- Three to ten keywords.
- Double spacing plus line and page numbering for submission.
- Editable Word, RTF, or TeX/LaTeX sources; Springer Nature LaTeX template is
  encouraged.
- Required `Declarations`: availability of data and materials, competing
  interests, funding, authors' contributions, acknowledgements, and optional
  authors' information.
- Cover letter explaining fit, policy issues, competing interests, author
  approval, and originality.
- Single-anonymous peer review.
- Fully open access; the journal page listed an APC of EUR 940 at the date above,
  subject to change and possible institutional coverage or waiver.
